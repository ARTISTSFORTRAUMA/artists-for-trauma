A Message from Artists for Trauma

July 23, 2012 · Level 1 Trauma

“Artists For Trauma wishes to extend our deepest condolences toward the victims and trauma survivors of Colorado theatre shooting. We hold a heavy heart for those who have lost precious loved ones in this terrible traumatic tragedy. AFT also extends great respect and gratitude to the emergency response organizations and individuals who bring their unique skill set and compassionate engagement to such a tragic and traumatic scene of life.”
