Movies and Meditation with NextStep Fitness

July 24, 2013 · Events · Level 1 Trauma · Recovery

Artists for Trauma and Next Step Fitness partnered again at their most recent Movie Night on Thursday the 18thof July to screen the documentary, “The Dhamma Brothers”. The film was followed by a short meditation session led by AFT Volunteer Artist Etan Boritzer.

NextStep members shared a few comments following the AFT meditation workshop:
