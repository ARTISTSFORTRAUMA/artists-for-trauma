AFT is offering festival passes to trauma survivors for the Rhythm and Joy (RAJ) Festival

September 27, 2013 · Artists · Events · News

Artists for Trauma is pleased to offer full festival passes to RAJ (Rhythm and Joy) Festival for twenty trauma survivors. The RAJ Festival is a three-day event celebrating art, music and community running October 4th-6th, 2013, at the beautiful Hummingbird Nest Ranch in Simi Valley, facilitated by Agape International Spiritual Center.

Performers include:

Chaka Khan

Common

Me’shell Ndegeocello

Luc & the Lovingtons

…and so many more!

We’d like to offer free full festival passes to the first 20 trauma survivors who express interest so they can take part in this three-day creative and spiritual event.

For more information about the festival, please visit the RAJ website.

To claim one of the twenty festival passes Artists for Trauma is offering, please contact Artists for Trauma on (424) 500-2422 or email at info@artistsfortrauma.org.
