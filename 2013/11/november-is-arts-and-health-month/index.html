November is Arts and Health Month

November 7, 2013 · News

We were so blessed to be highlighted in an article by Cultivating Culture in the context of November being Arts and Health Month.

The Global Alliance for Arts and Health (formerly the Society for the Arts in Healthcare) started Arts and Health Month to highlight the importance of using the arts for healing and recognizes the benefits of healing through art at a global level.

We couldn’t agree more!
