AFT and Topanga Terrace Partnership

February 11, 2015 · Events · News

Artists for Trauma, Topanga Terrace Rehabilitation and Subacute, and the Villas at Topanga Terrace, are partnering by offering workshops to residents of the Villas andTopanga Terrace Rehabilitation and Subacute to expedite recovery through artistic expression and human connection. The workshops will provide a creative portal to help patients process complex emotions, regain confidence, and build self-acceptance after suffering a traumatic experience.
