May is National Military Appreciation Month

May 5, 2015 · News

DEAR AFT FRIENDS, FAMILY AND SUPPORTERS,

EVERY DAY it is important to remember to appreciate the sacrifice and service the men and women of the U.S. Military make for each one of us as citizens of the United States at home and abroad.  DID YOU KNOW?…
MAY IS NATIONAL MILITARY APPRECIATION MONTH!

JOIN ARTISTS FOR TRAUMA in continuing to support our partner MILITARY CONNECTION, VAMBOA and all U.S. military troops, past and present in providing diverse, empowering and integrative resources to our veterans returning from military service to family and community life.

Support our continuing efforts to provide diverse, adaptive, empowering, healing resources to our veterans by donating to Artists for Trauma and/or Military Connection.

Thank you for helping us serve those that sacrifice and serve U.S.
