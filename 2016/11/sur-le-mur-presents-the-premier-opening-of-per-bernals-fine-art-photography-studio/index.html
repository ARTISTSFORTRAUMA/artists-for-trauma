Sur Le Mur presents the Premier Opening of Per Bernal’s Fine Art Photography Studio

November 5, 2016 · Events

Megan Phillips from Sur Le Mur is curating a great show that is benefitting Artists for Trauma with new works done by Per Bernal and RISK. There will be great art by Lincoln Townley, Billy Morrison, Plastic Jesus, Prince Lorenzo DeMedici, Alexandra Grant, Geoff Melville, Black Brain, Steve Kaufmann and Unfuk Yourself. Don’t forget to mark your calendars and R.S.V.P. to this amazing event!
