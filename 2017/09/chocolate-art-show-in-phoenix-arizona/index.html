Chocolate & Art Show in Phoenix, Arizona

September 14, 2017 · Events

Hello Artists for Trauma family and friends! If you are in Phoenix, please check out the Chocolate & Art Show on 9/14 – 9/15!
Indulge yourself in some of Phoenix’s finest up-and-coming artists, photographers and creators! There is something for every body including live body painting, live music, face-painting, and free chocolate!

This event will be held in Phoenix at “The Monorchid” on Thursday September 14th, and Friday September 15th.

The Monorchid
214 E. Roosevelt St.
Phoenix, AZ 85004
The show begin at 8PM and the show goes on until 2AM for both nights.
EARLY BIRD TICKET: : $10 (Will end 2 weeks before the show)
https://chocolateandartshowphoenix2017.eventbrite.com/
GENERAL ADMISSION:: $15 ONLINE
https://chocolateandartshowphoenix2017.eventbrite.com/
AT THE DOOR:: $20
See you there!
