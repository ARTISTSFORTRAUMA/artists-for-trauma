Jeff Hamilton Studio Presents Street Art Fair

September 2, 2017 · Events

935 Wall Street Los Angeles, CA 90015
SEP 2, 2017

Jeff Hamilton Studio appreciate all of the love and support you have given us as we continue to bring about new interest and exposure to the street art world! As a token of our appreciation and in celebration of Labor Day weekend we are holding a special one night only event.

FREE with RSVP!!!
Street Art Fair on Saturday September 2nd, 2017 7PM-1AM!

Located at 935 Wall Street Los Angeles,CA. 90015 in the heart of the #LAFashionDistrict Street Art Fair is an innovative new Art Exhibition giving the opportunity for emerging as well as established Street Artists to showcase their amazing works to the public! The Street Art Fair experience is a one night only extravaganza filled with Art, Live Performances, Music, Gourmet Food, Beverages, and Special Surprises you will not want to miss!

Featuring the works of some of the top Street Artists in the world including:
Ryan Fisher, Isaac Pelayo, Crystal Lyn, SEYXYES, Johnny Quintanilla, CADE, Svetlana Penrose, Tiquan Flowers, WritingsFromMichael, MR MK ULTRA, Jimmy Warhol, Sosa, M., M!SCRE8, Theodosia Marchant, Julia McMickle, Alyssa Newton, Kanaki, THRY, Johnny Otto, Alexander Romero, Snipt and Many More!

For free admission RSVP to streetartfairs@gmail.com (Limit of 4 guests per RSVP)

From all of us at Jeff Hamilton Studio thank you! We look forward to seeing you at Street Art Fair!

A portion of the proceeds go to benefit ARTISTS for TRAUMA and the Street Art Fair Initiatives!

We will also be holding a food drive for the Los Angeles Food Bank, please bring a canned good if you can!
