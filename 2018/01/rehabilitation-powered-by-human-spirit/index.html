Rehabilitation: Powered by Human Spirit

January 1, 2018 · Events · Workshops

Welcome to your physician office and healthcare team.
We hope you are inspired by these amazing artistic examples of the power of the human spirit. These empowering pieces were created by people who have traveled a journey similar to yours, recovering from life-altering trauma or illness. You are in the right place. We are here to support you and to enhance your physical, functional and emotional well-being. You are not alone. We hope you will be inspired and comforted by these works of art.
Project by: California Rehabilitation Medical Group and Artists for Trauma
