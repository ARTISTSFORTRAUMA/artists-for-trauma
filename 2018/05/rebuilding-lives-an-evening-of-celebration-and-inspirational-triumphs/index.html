Rebuilding Lives: An Evening of Celebration and Inspirational Triumphs

May 2, 2018 · Events · Workshops

A wonderful “Evening of Celebration and Inspirational Triumphs” spent with amazing people making a difference with the Triumph Foundation at the Kimpton Hotel Palomar. A couple of paintings from our Artists for Trauma artists, Cassandra Tang and Brandon Newcomer, were selected for the Triumph Foundation silent auction. The Artists for Trauma “Powered By Human Spirit” art gallery collection can be seen at California Rehabilitation Institute.
