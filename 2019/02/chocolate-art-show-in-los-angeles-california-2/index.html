Chocolate & Art Show in Los Angeles, California

February 22, 2019 · Events

Got Art? Got Chocolate?

We are back in Los Angeles! Indulge yourself in some of LA’s finest up-and-coming artists, photographers and creators! There is something for every body including live body painting, vendors, live music, face-painting, and free chocolate! Welcoming MedMen to the show!

This event will be held in Downtown Los Angeles at:

THE VORTEX
2341 E. Olympic Blvd.
Los Angeles, CA 90021

Be a part of a great cause – Portions of the profit will be donated to their non-profit partner, Artists For Trauma.
