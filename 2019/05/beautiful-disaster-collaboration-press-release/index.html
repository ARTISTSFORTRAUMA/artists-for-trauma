Beautiful Disaster Collaboration Press Release

May 9, 2019 · Events · Workshops

“With every fiber of clothing, it is the Beautiful Disaster mission to empower the Beautifully Broken, and Perfectly Imperfect.

Beautiful Disaster Clothing and Artists for Trauma are coming together for powerful collaboration on May 9, 2019.

AFT is dedicated to enriching the lives of both civilian and military trauma survivors through artistic expression and human connection.

This day will be about supporting women who have experienced physical and emotional trauma in their lives for a beautiful day of creativity, connectivity and community.

We look forward to revealing more about this incredible campaign…”

Artists for Trauma is honored and excited to collaborate with Beautiful Disaster Clothing and Wise Photography by Nicole. A portion of the proceeds from the clothing sale will go towards Artists for Trauma to continue to support our efforts of bringing art to those affected by trauma.
