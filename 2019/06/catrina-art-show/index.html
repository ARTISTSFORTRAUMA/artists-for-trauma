Catrina Art Show

June 22, 2019 · Events

A multicultural event dedicated to the Las Catrinas culture is happening this Saturday, June 22nd. Enjoy 50+ vendors, themed art, live music and much more! Catrina is dedicated to the Las Catrinas, day of the dead and the amazing culture that surrounds it.

Musical Guest Mariachi Divas, the two time Grammy award winning, will be there!
