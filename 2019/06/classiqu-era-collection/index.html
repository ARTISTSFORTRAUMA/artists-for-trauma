Classiqu’ Era Collection

June 1, 2019 · Events · Workshops

Classiqu’ Era and Artists for Trauma aligned for a soft launch and trunk show for the Classiqu’ Era Collection at a private home in Los Angeles.

“Born of desire to answer the decades old question asked by multitudes of women: ‘Why can’t I find a great dress’, Classic Era is dedicated to providing high quality, luxury, day-to-evening dresses for today’s sophisticated lady.”
