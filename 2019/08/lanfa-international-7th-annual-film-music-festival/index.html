LANFA International 7th Annual Film/Music Festival

August 4, 2019 · Events

The 7th Annual Film/Music Festival hosted by LANFA International will be held on August 4th at The Miracle Theatre.
226 S. Market St.
Inglewood, CA 90301

Sponsored by Los Angeles Nollywood Film/Music Association

Honoring Laura T. Sharpe for the Humanitarian Award, as well as Lilian Raven for the Inspiring Artist Award.
Congratulations to our community… Where Recovery is an Art!

“The Los Angeles Nollywood Film Association (LANFA) has experienced tremendous growth since it’s inception. LANFA is home for a multicultural event in which diversity is embraced and cherished. Over the years, our goal has been to provide a platform for our creative artists to produce and showcase their unique artistic abilities, increase the level of film and music awareness in the community, promote the brand, strengthen and grow the credibility of the group. To also foster the creation of successful working partnerships, empower networking, raise visibility, and strengthen distribution and marketing channels.”
