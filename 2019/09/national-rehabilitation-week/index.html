National Rehabilitation Week

September 24, 2019 · AFT

In honor of National Rehabilitation Week, Artists for Trauma delivered 59 floral arrangements for the staff, patients and guests of the California Rehabilitation Institute.

A big THANK YOU to AFT board member, Christina Noonan, for this generous donation to brighten our community in rehabilitation!
