Healing Arts Festival

November 7, 2019 · Events · Workshops

Associated Students of Moorpark College and Artists for Trauma present the Healing Arts Festival!

November 7, 2019
10am to 2pm
Upper Campus Quad at Moorpark College

Join us in commemorating the one year anniversary of the Borderline Shooting and the Woolsey Fire by exposing community members to healing through art and celebrating our community’s perseverance!

“To encourage artistic expression, the campus quad was turned into a makeshift arts classroom with areas for oil painting, spray painting, writing, drawing and music. Several students crowded around three chalk walls that read Love, Healing and Strength, and shared song lyrics, drawings and notes to Noel Sparks.” – Christina Cox
