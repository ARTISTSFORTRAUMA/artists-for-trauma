Operation Mend Thanksgiving Donation

November 27, 2019 · Events · Workshops

Thanks & Giving! Smart Art Box and Artists for Trauma’s November art supply donation went towards UCLAOperationMendand their support of our wounded warriors!

“Operation Mendprovides advanced surgical and medical treatment for post-9/11-era warriors injured during combat operations or while training for service, as well as comprehensive psychological healthcare and social support for both wounded warriors and their family members.”
