Chocolate and Art Show in San Francisco, CA

December 12, 2019 · Events

Got Art? Got Chocolate?

We left our hearts in San Francisco! Indulge yourself in some of the finest up-and-coming artists, photographers and creators! There is something for every body including live body painting, vendors, live music, face-painting, and free chocolate! This event will be held at:

SOMArts Cultural Center

934 Brannan St.

San Francisco, CA

Be a part of a great cause – Portions of the profit will be donated to their non-profit partner, Artists For Trauma.
