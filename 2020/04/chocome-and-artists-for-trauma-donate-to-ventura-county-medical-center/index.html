ChocoMe and Artists for Trauma Donate to Ventura County Medical Center

April 23, 2020 · Events

ChocoMe has donated $500 of their international award winning Voile and Entree chocolates to Artists For Trauma.

Flavors included are Ginger, Grapefruit, Lemon and Bergamot dipped in luxurious European dark chocolate. Additionally, bars of high quality nuts and fruits inlaid into divine, European dark chocolate.

AFT in turn donated them to our Ventura County community partners, Ventura County Medical Center and Health Care Foundation of Ventura County. Thank you to Amy Towner, CEO of HCFVC, for picking up the chocolates and gift packaging them to give to those who have given so much to others!
