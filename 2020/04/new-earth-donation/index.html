New Earth Donation

April 6, 2020 · Events · Workshops

Artists For Trauma and Smart Art Box donated to support New Earth by empowering their students through the arts, always and especially during these uncertain COVID19 physical distancing times.

Thank you to the leadership of AFT Director of Volunteer Artists, Melanie Gordon, for cultivating this new AFT community partnership!

“New Earth provides mentor-based arts, educational, and vocational programs that empower juvenile justice and system involved youth ages 13-25 to transform their lives, move toward positive, healthier life choices, and realize their full potential as contributing members of our community.”
