UPLIFT – A collaboration between Artists for Trauma and Infinite Flow Dance

June 13, 2020 · Events

Artists for Trauma is proud to support and sponsor “UPLIFT: Authentic stories from black creatives with disabilities” Saturday, June 13, 10:00-11:30am PST.

https://www.infiniteflowdance.org/uplift

The goal of UPLIFT is to:

– Uplift often overlooked voices from black people with disabilities.

– Give a platform for black creatives with disabilities to share their authentic stories and truth.

– Create an opportunity to listen, learn, and join the conversation.

Host: Stephanie Thomas

Panelists:

Tatiana Lee- Actress | Model | Hollywood Inclusion Influencer

Lauren “LoLo” Spencer- Disability Lifestyle Influencer

Shaheem Sanchez – Deaf Dancer | Social Media Influencer

Keeshelinooo- Dancer | Entertainer | Social Media Influencer

Wesley Hamilton- Founder, Disabled But Not Really | Guest, Queer Eye

Natalie- Blind Dancer | Diversity & Inclusion Consultant

Richard Bell– Visual Artist

(Panelists subject to change)

ASL Interpreter: Angelle Thomas

Organized by Marisa Hamamato

Inclusion Speaker | FounderInfinite Flow – An Inclusive Dance Company

ASL interpretation and closed captioning will be available.
