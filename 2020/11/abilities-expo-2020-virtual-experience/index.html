Abilities Expo 2020 Virtual Experience

November 20, 2020 · Events

We are so excited to be included in the list of fun things to do at the Abilities Expo Virtual Experience!

The online #AbilitiesVE2 is free on November 20-22, 2020.

This art event is great for all ages – gather old magazines, photos, found objects, glue and let’s see what we can create!

You can catch Zinnia Moreno at the virtual Abilities Expo workshop at 4pm on November 20, 2020!

“I’m proud to share how art helps me manage chronic illness and invite you to collage with me!”
