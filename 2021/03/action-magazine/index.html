Action Magazine

March 10, 2021 · Events

Action Magazine is an interactive entertainment and news experience “giving a worldwide platform for both adults and kids in the industry, who are passionate about taking a stance against bullying, a place to share their stories.”

In the March/April 2021 issue, “Next Best Thing NYC Cast” Special Edition, Laura Sharpe and Artists for Trauma are featured to discuss the beginnings of AFT, Laura’s triumph over tragedy and what is to come for our amazing community!
