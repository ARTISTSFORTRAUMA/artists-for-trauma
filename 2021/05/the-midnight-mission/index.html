The Midnight Mission

May 24, 2021 · Events

Artists for Trauma supports a community of survivors, thrivers and difference-makers through art and medical collaboration. We support The Midnight Mission and their belief that everyone deserves to live with dignity no matter what they’ve been through. The Midnight Mission’s multi-faceted approach to homeless services helps our community members make quality of life changes that last.

You can follow along with all the amazing work they do!
