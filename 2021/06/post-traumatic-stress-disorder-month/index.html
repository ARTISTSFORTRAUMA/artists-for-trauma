Post Traumatic Stress Disorder Month

June 28, 2021 · Events

June is Post Traumatic Stress Disorder Awareness Month

BrainLine.org is “is a national multimedia project offering authoritative information and support to anyone whose life has been affected by brain injury or PTSD.”

Our community partner, Josh O’Brien, is a war veteran and comedian who uses laughter to assist other veterans transitioning back into civilian life. His podcast, Civilianization, “is a show about those that did or are serving, lessons learned and the tales to be told… The mission in every episode is to be inform and entertain.”

Thank you to those like BrainLine.org and Josh O’Brien who are brave enough to come forward and share their experience! “PTSD is not a life sentence. Treatment can help someone experiencing PTSD reconnect with their family, friends and community.”
