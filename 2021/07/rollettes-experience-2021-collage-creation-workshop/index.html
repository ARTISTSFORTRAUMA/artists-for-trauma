Rollette’s Experience 2021 – Collage Creation Workshop

July 24, 2021 · Events

Artists for Trauma is proud to highlight Zinnia Moreno as she leads another creative workshop for our community. Zinnia and her 8 year old sister, Jazzy, will work with 20 young women to express their uniqueness by creating a collage masterpiece and learn new techniques about mixed media art!
