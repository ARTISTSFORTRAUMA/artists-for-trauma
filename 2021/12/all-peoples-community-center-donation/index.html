All Peoples Community Center Donation

December 19, 2021 · Events

Karen Michelle donated, through Artists For Trauma, 2,000 masks to All Peoples Community Center. All Peoples will include and distribute the marks during the four remaining 2021 family and community events – multiple designer child, tween and adult sizes for the entire family!
