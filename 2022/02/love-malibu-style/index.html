Love Malibu Style

February 5, 2022 · Events

Love Malibu Style benefiting Artists For Trauma, a 501 c3 non profit via a portion of all art sales.

Opening February 5, 2022 from 6pm to 9pm at Tracy Park Gallery

The 1st Annual Exhibition is brought to you by Eddie Donaldson, a.k.a. GuerillaOne, Jim “TAZ” Evans, Anne Martin of Sugar Press Art and Tracy Park Gallery. The show will feature a diverse array of Malibu based artists, showcasing their work across a variety of mediums and genres that best describes their own interpretation of LOVE MALIBU STYLE and their vision of civic pride. The exhibition will be on view at the Tracy Park Gallery located at the Malibu Lumber Yard, 3939 Cross Creek Road, Malibu, CA from February 5th to March 5th and online at SugarPressArt.com

“We are grateful for the leadership and community service activation of the Tracy Park Gallery, and participating talented artists.We feel truly blessed to be a part of the inaugural, Love Malibu Style event.

Thank you for supporting our shared mission to empower life altered, trauma survivors through diverse artistic expression, medical collaboration and human connection.”Laura Sharpe Artists For Trauma Founder, CEO

Since opening her doors in 2004, Tracy has been fervently dedicated to supporting both emerging and mid-career artists. Her inventory maintains a diverse collection from artists around the globe as well as a substantial amount from local artists.
