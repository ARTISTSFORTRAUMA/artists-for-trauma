HEAVEN ON PCH ~ A fundraiser for AFT

June 25, 2022 · Events

Heaven On PCH

A Fundraiser For Artists For Trauma

Saturday June 25th 2pm -7pm

21201 Pacific Coast Highway Malibu CA.

Please Join Beth Bowen and Eddie Donaldson for a Private Invitation Only day time event with ART, MUSIC and COLLABORATION. The intention for Heaven On PCH is to gather a group of artists,art collectors and art lovers for a day of CREATIVITY.CONNECTIVITY.COMMUNITY. A large portion of proceeds to benefit Artists For Trauma.

“Artists For Trauma is grateful to Beth Bowen and Eddie Donaldson for creatingHeaven on PCH! This creative, multi-media fundraising event is going to be an exciting, inclusive and accessible life experience for all attendees, trauma survivors, thrivers and difference makers!”

Laura T. Sharpe, Founder & CEO, Artists For Trauma

https://artistsfortrauma.org

‘Your life is your canvas! I’m excited to share my collection of “Your Life Your Canvas” with potential new collectors!:)”

Beth Bowen
