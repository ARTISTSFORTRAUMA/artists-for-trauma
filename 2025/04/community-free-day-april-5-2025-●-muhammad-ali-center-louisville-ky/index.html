Community Free Day (April 5, 2025 ● Muhammad Ali Center, Louisville, KY)

April 5, 2025 · Digital Flyers For Events

Join Artists for Trauma for Community Free Day on April 5, 2025, at the Muhammad Ali Center in Louisville, KY. Sponsored by Artists for Trauma, the Center will be open free to the public from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., removing barriers to access and inviting the community to explore Muhammad Ali’s life and legacy. This special day celebrates creativity, connection, and community through shared experience.
