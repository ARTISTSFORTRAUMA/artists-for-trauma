Aftershock Festival (October 2–5, 2025 ● Sacramento, CA)

January 26, 2026 · Digital Flyers For Events

Artists for Trauma will be part of the Aftershock Festival from October 2–5, 2025, in Sacramento, CA, offering interactive and adaptive healing experiences through art, sound, and creative expression. Guided by the belief that recovery is an art, these experiences are designed to support the mental, physical, and emotional well-being of survivors, thrivers, and difference-makers. Join us at 1600 Garden Highway, Sacramento, CA 95833, for a powerful blend of creativity, connection, and community during this transformative festival.
