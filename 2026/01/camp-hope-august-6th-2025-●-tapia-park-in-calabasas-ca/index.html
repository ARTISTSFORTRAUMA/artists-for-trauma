Camp Hope (August 6th, 2025 ● Tapia Park in Calabasas, CA)

January 26, 2026 · Digital Flyers For Events

Join Artists for Trauma for Camp Hope on August 6, 2025, at Tapia Park in Calabasas, CA. Artists for Trauma will facilitate two artist workshops for 80 children, working alongside Camp Hope counselors and staff to support a safe and creative environment. Programming will be led under the supervision of Mike Jump, Dina Zuhric, and other VCFJC staff, using art as a tool for connection, expression, and healing.
