LFA 210 (June 13, 2025 ● Freedom Hall, Louisville, KY)

January 26, 2026 · Digital Flyers For Events

Join Artists for Trauma at LFA 210 on June 13, 2025, at Freedom Hall in Louisville, KY. Artists for Trauma will be a featured sponsor, hosting an information table and a strong on-site presence, including signage in the octagon. Attendees will have the opportunity to learn about and experience adaptive self-defense youth programs designed to empower and educate through trauma-informed practices.
