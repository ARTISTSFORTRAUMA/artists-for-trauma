Painted Toes: Adaptive Multi-Media Painting Workshop (March 15, 2025 ● California Rehabilitation Institute)

January 25, 2026 · Digital Flyers For Events

Join Artists for Trauma for Painted Toes, an adaptive multi-media painting workshop and fundraising event on March 15, 2025, at the California Rehabilitation Institute in Los Angeles. Led by Laura Sharpe, Aaron Baker, and Laquita Dian, this event creates a safe, creative space that fosters healing, connection, and community through art.
