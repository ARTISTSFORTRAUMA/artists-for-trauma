State of the Art (April 26, 2025 ● Hermosa Beach, CA)

January 25, 2026 · Digital Flyers For Events

Join Artists for Trauma for State of the Art, a fundraising event on April 26, 2025, at Indivisible Arts in Hermosa Beach, CA. This annual celebration brings together creativity, connection, and community for one unforgettable evening. Centered on Universal Language, the event honors communication and expression that transcend words through art.
