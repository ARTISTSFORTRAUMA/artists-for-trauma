The Art of Healing (April 12, 2025 ● Ice House, Louisville, KY)

January 25, 2026 · Digital Flyers For Events

Join Artists for Trauma for The Art of Healing, a fundraising event on April 12, 2025, at the Ice House in Louisville, KY. This special evening celebrates creativity as a pathway to recovery and community. In support of the event, Artists for Trauma is donating 200 MuseKit Art Swag Bags filled with creative tools and resources to empower healing through art.
