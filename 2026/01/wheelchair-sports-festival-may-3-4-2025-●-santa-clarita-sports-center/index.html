Wheelchair Sports Festival (May 3–4, 2025 ● Santa Clarita Sports Center)

January 26, 2026 · Digital Flyers For Events

Artists for Trauma will be part of the Triumph Foundation Wheelchair Sports Festival on May 3–4, 2025, at the Santa Clarita Sports Center in Santa Clarita, CA. The festival features more than 15 activities, including AFT adaptive art workshops, creating space for connection, creativity, and community. Open to all skill levels, this inclusive weekend is designed to empower participants to explore their abilities, build meaningful relationships, and celebrate resilience together at 25385 Rye Canyon Rd., Santa Clarita, CA 91355.
