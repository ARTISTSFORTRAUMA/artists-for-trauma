5th Annual Adaptive Sports Expo

2019-10-11

5th Annual Adaptive Sports Expo sponsored by Long Beach VA.  This showcase is organized to connect Veterans and health care practitioners allowing them to see, participate and and in engage in Adaptive Sports and other activities. AFT will engage participants in a Graffiti Pop-Up art project facilitated by well-known artists.
