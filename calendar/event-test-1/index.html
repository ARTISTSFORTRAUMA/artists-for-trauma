Healing Art Festival

2019-11-07 · Moorpark College, Moorpark, CA

Festival is supporting students affected by the Woolsey Fire, and the Borderline Shooting emotionally, mentally and/or physically.  AFT will provide speakers who have experienced trauma and engage participants in a Graffiti Pop-Up art project facilitated by well- known artists.
