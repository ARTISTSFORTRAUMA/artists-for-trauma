C.O.R.E. – Center Of Restorative Exercise

C.O.R.E. is a state-of-the-art, adaptive fitness facility that provides health and wellness benefits of ongoing Restorative Exercise. They are equipped with unique and specialized adaptive equipment to accommodate diverse rehabilitative needs with the goal of improve function, independence, and self-efficacy.
