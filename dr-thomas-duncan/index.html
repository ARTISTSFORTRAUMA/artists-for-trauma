Dr. Thomas Duncan

Dr. Thomas Duncan is Co-Director of Anacapa Surgery Associates in Ventura, California and is Co-Trauma Medical Director of Ventura County Medical Center. He is an Advanced Trauma Life Support Instructor, Rural Trauma Life Support Course Director, and Stop The Bleed Course Director.

He received his medical degree from Chicago College of Osteopathic Medicine and has been in practice for more than 20 years.

Dr. Thomas Duncan has been an Artists for Trauma board member since 2012
