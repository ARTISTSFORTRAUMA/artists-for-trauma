Laura Sharpe's Artist Profile

Every recovery is a story, and every story is a work of art. These features celebrate the Survivors, Thrivers and Difference Makers of the Artists for Trauma community: trauma survivors, artists, athletes, surgeons and advocates who have turned life-altering moments into creativity, connection and purpose.

Through in-depth interviews with Artists for Trauma, our Fellow Travelers share how art, movement and human connection carried them forward, physically, mentally and emotionally, and how they now light the way for others. Creativity. Connectivity. Community. Powered by human spirit™.
