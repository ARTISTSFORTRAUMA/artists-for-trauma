Tobias Forrest’s path into storytelling began after a life-altering spinal cord injury resulting in quadriplegia.

In that moment of providence, strangers came together to save his life.

In early recovery, he turned to painting as a way to process, sparking a creativity that would expand across multiple art forms.

PsychologyRebuild & reinvent

As he began to rebuild and reinvent, Tobias pursued a Master’s degree in psychology.

Not just studied, but lived. A foundation that would shape his work as both an artist and an advocate.

“Developing a deeper understanding of people, of emotion, of behavior.”

First auditionA turning point

An assigned performance in class left the audience struck by Tobias’ natural presence and ability to connect.

That moment led to his first audition, earning him the Christopher Reeve Acting Scholarship. A turning point that set a new direction in entertainment in motion.

“Entertainment is advocacy. It is education. It is inspiration. It creates conversations around diversity, disability, inclusion, and awareness.”
Tobias Forrest
“There is talent out there that can do the job. The industry needs to trust that.”
Tobias Forrest
For Tobias, the issue is not capability. It is access.

Representation is not just who is seen. It is who is creating, who is directing, who is part of the process.

“Include disability, not just in front of the camera, but behind it too.”
Tobias Forrest

Artists for TraumaA tool for healing

His intersection with Laura Sharpe and Artists for Trauma reflects that same belief. Art is not only a platform of expression, but a tool for healing.

Tobias with Laura Sharpe & Karen Michelle at Lybevents Bridal and Special Event Expo at Luxe Sunset Blvd Hotel.

The paintingsAn early chapter resurfaced

Through that connection, an early chapter of Tobias’s recovery journey resurfaced.

The paintings created during early stages of his recovery were featured in a museum-quality installation at California Rehabilitation Institute, sharing that healing beyond himself.

Film, TV, music & stageRange & versatility

Across film, television, music, and stage, Tobias Forrest has built an impressive body of work defined by range and versatility.

With credits including Law & Order: Organized Crime, How to Get Away with Murder, Mr. Crocodile, Daruma (2023), FBI Most Wanted (2025), GOAT (2026) and I Play Rocky (2026), as well as stage performances in Cost of Living, Colossal, and Pyretown.

He is also the lead singer of the band Cityzen, extending his storytelling beyond the screen.

Media Access AwardsFull circle

Two decades after his first acting opportunity, Tobias received the Christopher Reeve Acting Award at the Media Access Awards for his role as Patrick in Daruma (2023).

A full-circle moment rooted in resilience, advocacy, and purpose, his work continues to grow and evolve as Tobias expands the many dimensions of his multihyphenate career.
