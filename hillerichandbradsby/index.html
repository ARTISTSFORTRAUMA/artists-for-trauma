Hillerich & Bradsby Co.

In 1884 we started making baseball bats. Fast forward to now, Hillerich and Bradsby Co. is in its fifth generation of continuous family ownership. Our campus, located in downtown Louisville, KY, is adorned with the largest baseball bat in the world. It houses our world-famous Louisville Slugger Museum & Factory and our newly opened Barrels and Billets, where you can create your own custom bourbon.
