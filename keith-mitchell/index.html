Keith Mitchell

In 2014, Keith founded The Light It Up Foundation, a 501c3 non-profit.

Through his charity and altruistic endeavors he initiated the KM59 Wellness Movement which is intentionally changing the lives of young people (including inner-city kids), trauma survivors, first responders and U.S. war veterans. Keith works with communities, schools and organizations to introduce mindfulness, meditation and movement (though Yoga).

Through encouraging consistent, self-empowerment practices, Keith is committed to his mission of inspiring personal transformation in the lives of millions.
