Kent Seton

Kent E. Seton is the Acting CEO and Founder of the Edward Charles Foundation (ECF) which operates 60 charitable initiatives around the World. A few of the initiatives operated include raising funds and building a University in Rwanda for scientists to study the extinct species of Mountain Gorilla, assisting in awarding grants and scholarships to young female tech entrepreneurs attempting to solve some of the world’s biggest problems like Alzheimer’s, helping launch a national initiative to help provide a voice for the voiceless in America’s prison system, a social enterprise to help survivors of human trafficking to find meaning and job opportunity and finally to help fight HIV Aids in Africa. Kent is an experienced traveler and has been to over 50 countries. He has taken mission trips to Cuba, Morocco and Dominican Republic to name a few.
