Kimberly Rath

Kimberly Rath has over 25 years of experience in human resources and is a recognized leader in the field of executive development and human resources in the United States and Asia. Demonstrating a strong commitment to others, Rath helps clients reach their highest growth potential through talent-based strategies. As Talent Plus’ chairman, president and co-founder, she has driven the company’s growth that now includes an office not only in Lincoln, Nebraska, but also in Singapore.

Among others, Rath was highlighted in the 2011 book, “Prescription for Excellence.” In 2013, Rath was presented the Business Excellence Award from the University of Nebraska – Lincoln and the Delta Gamma Shield Award (a national award). Prior to founding Talent Plus, Rath worked with The Gallup Organization for seven years. She is a graduate of the University of Nebraska – Lincoln.
