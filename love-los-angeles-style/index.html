Love Los Angeles Style

Benefiting Artists For Trauma, a 501 c3 non profit via a portion of all art sales.

The 3rd Annual Exhibition is brought to you by Eddie Donaldson, a.k.a. GuerillaOne, Jim “TAZ” Evans, and Anne Martin of Sugar Press Art. The show will feature a diverse array of LA based artists, showcasing their work across a variety of mediums and genres that best describes their own interpretation of LOVE LA STYLE and their vision of civic pride for Los Angeles.
