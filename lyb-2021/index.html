Artists For Trauma partners with Karen Michelle for the LYB21 Event

October 3rd, 2021

An afternoon of fun with a Getty Image covered red carpet, fabulous fashion runway show featuring established and new emerging designers, shopping from all of our fantastic vendors, and photo-op fun. Love Your Body Events are about self esteem & empowerment of women & girls of all shapes, backgrounds, sizes & ages! A place where everyone is welcome!

A new addition for 2021 was the Artists in the Garden Art Auction with proceeds benefiting the building of AFT “Artists Retreat” at the Risk Rock Studios in Thousand Oaks. Collaborating with Kelly “Risk” Graval, this signature brick and mortar art studio will help adaptive artists recover from life altering traumas.

We are proud to announce Deborah Alessi, CEO/Founder of Face Forward, Inc., received our prestigious Karen Michelle Heart Award for outstanding contribution to the community. The Heart Award was presented by our previous recipient, Laura T. Sharpe CEO/Founder of Artists for Trauma.
