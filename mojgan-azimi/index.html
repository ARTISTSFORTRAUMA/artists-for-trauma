Mojgan Azimi

Mojgan Azimi is an internationally renowned cosmetic, reconstructive and paramedical permanent make-up artist.

She has over twenty years of experience as one of the leading experts in this delicate field, and has been endorsed by world-class plastic surgeons and dermatologists.

Ms Azimi holds a four-year degree in Make-Up Design for Theater, Cinema and TV from a leading international academy. She has devoted decades to the study of facial structure, anatomy, color theory, the psychology of the face, and other fields that make her one of the best trained practitioners in her field.

All this knowledge is combined with an innate sense of artistry to support the very best results for clients that come to her from all over the globe.

This extends to the use of her gifts in assisting cosmetic clients, cancer patients, those suffering with alopecia, vitiligo, burns and accidents.

Her goal is to help every client to feel confident and at ease with their appearance.
