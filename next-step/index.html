Next Step

Nationally Recognized Non-Profit

NextStep, a nationally recognized non-profit organization, believes that all persons living with paralysis and other physical disabilities deserve the hope and opportunity to live long and healthy lives. NextStep is a nationwide movement that aims to revolutionize the quality of healthcare for the physically challenged community.
