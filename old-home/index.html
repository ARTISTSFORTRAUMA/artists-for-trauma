Who We Serve

Civilian and military survivors of all ages; impacted by life-altering traumas

Our Community

What We Do

We provide interactive, and adaptive healing through art, music, & wonder experiences to empower the mental, physical and emotional well being of survivors.  We collaborate with scientific and medical research in the field of life altering trauma, including areas of physical, mental, sexual and emotional disfigurement and disability and other types of traumatic events beyond personal control.

Learn More

Our Partners

We partner with local to internationally established artists, businesses, municipalities, trauma hospitals, post-acute rehab facilities, all types of trauma support groups, non-profits, military resource centers and individual trauma survivors
