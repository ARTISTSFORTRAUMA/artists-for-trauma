Rhythm And Joy Festival

Annual Transformative Music Festival

The Rhythm and Joy Festival (RAJ) is a soulful celebration demonstrating the impact of music as a catalyst for changing lives. This transformative experience brings together iconic music and performing artists, innovative speakers and socially responsible entrepreneurs. Our goal is to create an atmosphere where festival-goers can truly meet each other, learn new ways to live healthier lives, and be inspired by music and activities that have progressive and uplifting messages.
