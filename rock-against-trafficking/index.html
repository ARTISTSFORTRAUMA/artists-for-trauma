Rock Against Trafficking

Rock Against Trafficking is a non-profit benefit corporation organized to amplify the power of music to end human trafficking. A coalition of Grammy-winning and national touring artists are collaborating with Rock Against Trafficking.

Artists for Trauma stands in alignment with Rock Against Trafficking to Support & Heighten Awareness through the Power of Entertainment, Music, Arts and as platforms of healing against life altering traumas.
