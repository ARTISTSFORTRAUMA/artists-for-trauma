Sunny Chayes – Singin’ In The Rain

Media Personality & Global Humanitarian

Sunny Chayes has worked passionately in The Peace Movement, The Environmental Movement, as well as The Global Humanitarian and Productive Change Movement for many years. Sunny has dedicated her life to carry on the work of her family. The Chayes family and the Shapiros were highly influential in politics, environmental accountability, the arts, philanthropy and peaceful conflict resolution policy.
