Jim Evans

Also known as TAZ, Californian Jim Evans has been creating poster and album art for the likes of Foo Fighters, U2, Oasis, Green Day, Pearl Jam, Beastie Boys, L7, Neil Young, Beck, Aerosmith, Ramones, Rage Against the Machine, Wu Tang Clan, Metallica, Anderson .Paak and Nine Inch Nails. Cutting his teeth in the underground rock scene in San Francisco during 1967’s Summer of Love, he left being a guitarist in a rock band to concentrate on his comic books, flyers and underground art, before moving into advertising and magazine illustrations, creating movie posters and titles and, of course, working with some of the biggest rock bands on the planet.
